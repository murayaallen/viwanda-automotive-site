What we do.

Eight services, and what each one actually involves. Every job is quoted on assessment — a figure given before anyone has looked at the car is a guess, and we would rather not start there.

    Tunakuja kwako We come to you

    The car does not have to find us.

    Office and corporate clients get pick-up and drop-off. Everyone else gets the mobile service: door to door, wherever the car is.

    Maswali Questions

    What people ask first.

    Can you quote from a photo?

    For most paint and panel work, yes. Send photos on WhatsApp — the whole side of the car first, then close-ups of the damage — and we come back with a figure. Some jobs need the car in the bay before anyone can honestly put a number on them, and when that is the case we say so.

    Do you work with insurers?

    Yes. Accident repair is most of what insurers send us, and the assessor gets the same stage-by-stage photographs the owner does. Resolution Insurance is among the organisations that have sent vehicles here.

    How long does a respray take?

    It depends on how much preparation the panels need, which is why the date comes with the figure rather than before it. Stripping is where a job gets longer or shorter, and if it changes you hear about it then, not at handover.

    Will the colour match the rest of the car?

    We work from the vehicle’s colour code and refinish to the original, two-tone included. The Honda CR-V on our before-and-after page is one example: the repair does not announce itself.

    Can you get parts for my model?

    For any model. Parts are sourced abroad and cleared through Mombasa, which takes the time it takes — what we can do is tell you where the part is and when it lands, instead of leaving you to guess.

    Can you code an imported module or a replacement key?

    Yes. Reprogramming after a module or engine swap, immobiliser and key coding, and sensor calibration. A replacement part that has not been coded to the vehicle is a part that does not work, and that is where most imported-module jobs go wrong.

    Where are you?

    Garden Estate, off Thika Road, Nairobi. Call or WhatsApp ahead before you come and we will make sure someone is ready for you.

    Can you collect the car from our office?

    Yes. For office and corporate clients we pick the vehicle up and drop it back when the work is done. Send the location on WhatsApp and we arrange the collection.

    Do you come to the car?

    Yes. The mobile service is door to door, wherever you are. What can be done on the spot is done there; what needs the bay, we take in.

    Not sure which of these it is?

    Send a photo on WhatsApp. Working out what a car actually needs is the first part of the job.
